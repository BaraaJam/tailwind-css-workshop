The diploma uses the [Tabler Icons certificate](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/blob/main/icons/outline/certificate.svg), under the MIT license in TABLER-LICENSE. The local version crops unused space and replaces the stroke colour with the cap's purple, pink, and yellow palette.

The cap, arrow, and question-mark vectors were drawn for this project. The renderer samples the local artwork into cached ASCII glyphs.
