The diploma adapts the [Tabler Icons certificate](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/blob/main/icons/outline/certificate.svg), under the MIT license in TABLER-LICENSE. The local version uses the cap's purple, pink, and yellow palette, a thinner outline, transparent paper, simplified text lines, and a separate seal with clear space around it for ASCII sampling.

The cap, arrow, and question-mark vectors were drawn for this project. The renderer samples the local artwork into cached ASCII glyphs.
